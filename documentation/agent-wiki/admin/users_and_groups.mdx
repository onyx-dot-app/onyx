Two sections under **Admin → Permissions**: **Users & Requests** for individual accounts,
and **Groups** for permission groups.

## Users

The first account created on a new instance becomes the admin. After that,
how people get accounts depends on your [authentication configuration](/agent-wiki/deployment/configuration)
— email and password by default, or OIDC against your identity provider.
`ALLOWED_EMAILS` restricts who may sign up at all.

Admins bypass page-level permission checks, so keep the admin set small.

## Groups and page permissions

Groups are how you grant access to parts of the wiki without naming individuals on every page.
Permissions are stored per path, and a grant on a folder cascades to everything beneath it.
But unlike [update policies](/agent-wiki/using/update_policies), grants are **additive, with no deny**:
a more specific grant can open up extra access on one page,
but you can't grant a folder and then carve a single page out of it. When part of a folder needs to stay restricted,
grant at the narrower scopes from the start.

## Permissions reach further than the page

Worth understanding before you restrict anything,
because access controls affect features that aren't obviously about access:

- **Search** results are filtered to what you can read.
- **Agents** act as the user whose API key they hold, so an agent sees exactly what that person sees — an admin's key
  makes an admin's agent.
- **Auto Organize** never pairs pages across a visibility boundary, so a proposal can't reveal that a restricted page
  exists. A duplicate spanning a boundary that nobody can see both sides of simply goes undetected.
- **Triggers** require read access to everything they watch.
