Two delivery channels an admin configures once,
after which users can point their own [triggers](/agent-wiki/automate/triggers) at them.

## Slack app

Under **Admin → Slack App**,
save the OAuth credentials for the Agent Wiki Slack app — found under **Basic Information → App Credentials** on
`api.slack.com`.

The **Connect Slack** flow stays hidden from users until both credentials are saved. Once they are,
each person connects their own Slack account from **Settings → Connectors**,
and can then send trigger fires to any channel they can post to, or as a DM.

Individual users can **Mute** their connection to pause Slack delivery without disconnecting — fires are still recorded
in the Activity Center.

## Outbound email

Under **Admin → Outbound Email**, configure the SMTP account every email the wiki sends goes through:
trigger notifications, verification links, and notification emails.

Sending stays **off** until a host and a from-address are saved. Until then,
email verification links won't arrive either — worth knowing if you're standing up an instance with email-based sign-up
and wondering why nobody can confirm their address.

## Related

Both channels appear as destinations when creating a trigger, alongside the Activity Center, webhooks, and Onyx Craft.
See [Triggers](/agent-wiki/automate/triggers).
