Wikis decay. Duplicate pages accumulate, folders empty out, a page gets created twice under slightly different names.
**Auto Organize** scans for that clutter and proposes fixes — it never reorganizes silently behind you.

Everything it finds becomes a **proposal** you approve or reject. Nothing is applied without consent,
except in scopes you have explicitly marked as AI-managed.

## What it looks for

A sweep detects structural problems with definite answers, such as:

- **Empty folders** left behind after their contents moved
- **Duplicate pages** with identical content, proposing a merge into one survivor
- **Name collisions** that differ only by capitalization
- **Unfilled templates** — a page still identical to the template it was created from
- **Stub pages** that never got written
- **Deeply nested single-child folders** that can be flattened

Because deletion goes to [Trash](/agent-wiki/using/trash) with a 30-day recovery window,
approving a cleanup is not a one-way door.

## Reviewing proposals

When something is proposed for a page or folder you own, you'll see it in two places:

- A **banner on the page or folder** itself, with approve and reject.
- The **Suggestions** card in a folder's side panel, listing everything pending underneath it.

Each proposal describes the change in plain terms and, where it applies, shows a preview.
You approve the *intent* — the wiki then applies it against the page's current state,
so an unrelated edit made while the proposal was waiting doesn't invalidate it.

Rejecting is durable: the same finding is never proposed again. It's the way to say "this duplicate is deliberate."

## Turning it on for a scope

Auto Organize only touches a page or folder when its **Organize** switch is on — see [Update
Policies](/agent-wiki/using/update_policies). With it off, detectors skip the scope entirely.

With it on, proposals in that scope can be applied automatically rather than waiting in your queue. That's the trade:
the switch is how you say "this area is tidy-able without asking me each time."

## Admin controls

Under **Admin → Auto Organize**:

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Auto Organize** | The master switch. Off means no sweeps run, nothing is auto-applied, and pending proposals are frozen |
| **Automatic sweeps** | Off, Daily, or Weekly — scheduled whole-wiki scans at a fixed off-peak time (UTC) |
| **Scan the whole wiki** | Runs a sweep immediately |
| **Recent sweeps** | What the last runs scanned and proposed, including failures |

A sweep also runs automatically, narrowly scoped,
when a page is created — that's what catches a duplicate or a name collision at the moment it's introduced,
rather than days later.

<Note>
  The master switch is a genuine freeze, not a pause on new work.
  Turning it off also stops pending proposals from being applied, so it's the right lever if something looks wrong.
</Note>

## Good to know

- **Permissions are respected throughout.** A proposal never pairs pages across a visibility boundary, so a suggestion
  can't reveal that a restricted page exists.
- **Only one live proposal per page.** Findings are de-duplicated, so a page can't accumulate competing suggestions or
  re-nag you every sweep.
- **Auto-applied changes are visible.** They appear in the Activity Center, and every change is an ordinary commit in
  the page's history.
