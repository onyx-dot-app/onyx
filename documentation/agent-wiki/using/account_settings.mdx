Personal preferences, scoped to you.
Organization-wide configuration is in the [admin console](/agent-wiki/admin/overview) instead.

Open **Settings** from the sidebar.

| Section | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | Your full name and work role |
| **Appearance** | Light or dark theme |
| **Wiki** | Your timezone and default landing view |
| **Notifications** | Comment emails and auto-update warning emails |
| **Connectors** | Connect Slack, and manage email recipients |
| **Account** | Your email address |

Two of these matter more than the rest:

**Timezone** is what [scheduled triggers](/agent-wiki/automate/triggers) run against,
and what timestamps in the Activity Center are displayed in. Set it before building schedules,
or a daily trigger will fire at an hour you didn't intend.

**Connectors** is where you connect your own Slack account, once an admin has saved the Slack app credentials.
The connection is per-user: your triggers post as you, to channels you can post to.
**Mute** pauses Slack delivery without disconnecting, and fires are still recorded.
