Deleting in Agent Wiki is reversible. A deleted page or folder moves to **Trash**,
where it stays recoverable for **30 days** before being removed automatically.

Open **Trash** from the left sidebar to see everything deleted, with two actions per item:

- **Restore** — moves it back to its original location.
- **Delete permanently** — removes it immediately, before the 30 days are up.

## Why this matters more than usual

Trash is what makes the rest of the product safe to automate. Because deletion is reversible:

- Agents connected over [MCP](/agent-wiki/automate/mcp) are allowed to delete pages at all — `delete_doc` is a trash
  move, not a destructive one.
- [Auto Organize](/agent-wiki/automate/auto_organize) can act on approved cleanups without needing to be perfect,
  since a wrong removal is one click from undone.

Folder deletion is a deliberate exception: agents can delete pages, not folders. The blast radius is larger,
so that stays a human action.

## What happens to a deleted page

A trashed page is dropped from search and from the live wiki, and its permissions, comments,
and update policy follow it to the trash location rather than being discarded. Restoring puts all of it back together.

Links and identity are handled too. Every page carries a stable id behind its path,
so a restored page is the same page — not a lookalike at the same location.

## Good to know

- **30 days is a floor, not a promise of visibility.** An item removed after the window is gone from Trash; recovering
  it then means restoring from a backup.
- **A page recreated at a deleted page's path is a new page.** It gets a fresh identity rather than inheriting the
  deleted one's history, so restoring the original later doesn't merge the two.
- **History survives regardless.** The wiki is git-backed, so even a permanently deleted page's past commits remain in
  the repository's history.
