## How it works

The Google Chat connector indexes the messages of the named spaces in your Google Workspace.
The messages of one space on one day (UTC) make one document, titled with the space name and the date.
A document holds at most the first 10,000 messages of its day; later messages of that day are not indexed.
In each document, a thread's replies are indented under the thread's first message of that day.
Attachment names are indexed. Attachment content is not.

| Content     | Notes                                                                                                   |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Spaces      | Every named space in the organization, also spaces that no Onyx user is a member of.                   |
| Scope       | Optional. Limit the index to specific spaces by name or by id.                                          |
| Not indexed | Direct messages, group chats, and messages with neither text nor an attachment (for example, card-only messages from Chat apps). |

When a new message arrives, Onyx indexes the whole day of that message again.
An edit or a deletion of an older message shows in the index only after a new message arrives on the same day.

## Setting up

The connector uses a Google service account with domain-wide delegation. A Google Workspace is required.
Onyx lists the spaces and their members as a Google Workspace admin.
It then reads the messages of each space as one of the space's members.

### Authorization

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Google Cloud project">
    Go to [https://console.cloud.google.com/projectcreate](https://console.cloud.google.com/projectcreate)
    and create a project. You can also use the project of your Gmail or Google Drive service account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable the Google Chat API and the Admin SDK API">
    - In the left panel, open **APIs & services** and go to **Enabled APIs and services**.
    - Click **+ ENABLE APIS AND SERVICES**.
    - Search for **Google Chat API** and click **ENABLE**.
    - Search for **Admin SDK API** and click **ENABLE**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a service account">
    - Open the [service accounts page](https://console.cloud.google.com/iam-admin/serviceaccounts) in Google Cloud.
    - Click **Create Service Account** and complete step 1. Skip steps 2 and 3.
    - Open the **Keys** section and click **Add Key**. Download the JSON key.

    For Google organizations created after April 2024, allow key creation first:
    - Open this [policy page](https://console.cloud.google.com/iam-admin/orgpolicies/iam-disableServiceAccountKeyCreation).
    - Select **Manage**, then **Override parent's policy**, then **Not enforced** under **Rules**.
    - Select **SET POLICY**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Grant domain-wide delegation">
    Give the service account read-only access to Google Chat:
    - Copy the **Unique ID** of the service account.
    - Open the [Domain-wide Delegation page](https://admin.google.com/ac/owl/domainwidedelegation) in the Google Admin
    console.
    - Click **Add new** and enter the **Unique ID** as the client ID.
    - Paste this list into **OAuth scopes**:
    `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/chat.admin.spaces.readonly,https://www.googleapis.com/auth/chat.admin.memberships.readonly,https://www.googleapis.com/auth/chat.messages.readonly,https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.user.readonly`
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the primary admin">
    The primary admin is the Google Workspace user that Onyx lists spaces and members as.
    It needs an admin role with the **Users > Read** privilege and the Google Chat privileges to manage Chat and spaces
    conversations. Set the role in the Google Admin console under **Account > Admin roles**.
    A super admin has these privileges.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Indexing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the account">
    In the Admin Panel, go to **Connectors** and select **Google Chat**. Select **New Google Chat Account**:

    - **Primary Admin Email**: the email of the primary admin. Do not enter the service account email.
    - **Service Account Key**: upload or paste the JSON key you downloaded.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose what to index">
    Leave **Spaces** empty to index every named space. To narrow the index, enter space names,
    or space ids from the space URL. In the URL `https://mail.google.com/chat/u/0/#chat/space/AAAAbCdEfGh`,
    the space id is `AAAAbCdEfGh`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose who can see the messages">
    Under **Document Access**, select who can see the indexed messages, then click **Create Connector**.
    Google Chat has no permission sync. With **Public**, every Onyx user can find the messages of every space.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem                                                     | Fix                                                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The service account is not authorized for the scopes        | Delegate all four scopes to the service account's **Unique ID**. A change to domain-wide delegation can take some minutes to apply.   |
| The primary admin cannot list Google Chat spaces            | Give the primary admin the Google Chat privileges to manage Chat and spaces conversations, or use a super admin.                     |
| No spaces match the **Spaces** entries                      | Use the exact space name or the space id from the space URL.                                                                          |
| A space shows as an indexing error | Onyx reads a space as a member who has joined it and is not suspended. Make sure the space has such a member and that Google Chat is on for its members. |
