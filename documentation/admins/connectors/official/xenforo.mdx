## How it works

The XenForo connector scrapes posts from a XenForo v2.2 forum. You give it a board URL or a thread URL.
Onyx groups the posts of a thread into documents and links each document back to the thread.

Each run re-reads the whole board or thread. There is no incremental update.

The connector reads public pages only. It needs no credential.

Permission sync is not available. **Document Access** can be **Everyone in Your Organization** or **Specific Groups**.
**Specific Groups** is a paid feature: the Business and Enterprise tiers on Onyx Cloud,
and the Enterprise Edition when self-hosted.

The connector is available in all editions: Community Edition, Business, Enterprise, and Onyx Cloud.

## Setting up

### Authorization

The forum pages must be reachable from the Onyx server without a login.

### Indexing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the URL">
    In the Admin Panel, go to **Connectors** and select **XenForo**. In **URL**,
    enter the board URL or thread URL to index.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set access and create">
    Under **Connector Settings**, choose **Document Access** and click **Create Connector**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing the connector

Because every run re-reads the forum, keep the refresh schedule long for large boards.
