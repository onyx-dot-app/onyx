## How it works

The Asana connector indexes tasks from one Asana workspace. You can index every project in the workspace,
or only the projects you list.

Each task becomes one document. It holds the task name, description, assignee, due date, custom fields,
and every comment on the task. Archived projects are skipped.

Onyx pulls new and changed tasks every 30 minutes by default. You can change this under **Schedule**.

**Sync with Asana** is not offered.
The access type can only be **Everyone in Your Organization** or **Specific Groups**.

## Setting up

### Authorization

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Personal Access Token">
    - Log in to Asana and go to https://app.asana.com/0/my-apps
    - Click **Create new token**
    - Give it a name, such as `Onyx`, and create the token
    - Copy the token.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Indexing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect an Asana account">
    In Onyx, go to **Admin Panel → Connectors** and select **Asana**. Under **Authentication Account**,
    select **New Asana Account** and paste the Personal Access Token into **API Token**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose what to index">
    - **Workspace ID**: Your Asana workspace ID. Find it at https://app.asana.com/api/1.0/workspaces.
    It is a number such as `1234567890123456`.
    - **Project IDs** (optional): To index specific projects, enter their IDs separated by commas,
    for example `1234567890123456,2345678901234567`. Leave it empty to index every project in the workspace.
    To find a project ID, open the project in Asana.
    In a URL such as `https://app.asana.com/0/1208338159336610/1208338159510597`, `1208338159336610` is the project ID.
    - **Team ID** (optional): A team whose private projects Onyx may index in addition to public projects.
    When you set a Team ID and leave **Project IDs** empty, private projects outside that team are skipped.
    Projects you list under **Project IDs** are always indexed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose who can see the tasks">
    Under **Document Access**, select **Everyone in Your Organization** or **Specific Groups**.
    **Specific Groups** is available on Onyx Cloud and Enterprise Edition.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create the connector">
    Select **Create Connector**. Open **Admin Panel → Indexing Status**, select the connector,
    and confirm its first indexing attempt completes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The connector indexes only what the token's Asana user can see.
Make sure that user has access to every project you want to index.
