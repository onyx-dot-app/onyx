## How it works

The Gmail connector indexes the email threads in the mailboxes of your Google Workspace.
Each thread becomes one document that links to the thread in Gmail.
Onyx indexes the plain-text part of each message and keeps the subject, sender, recipients, date,
and labels as metadata. It skips the **Spam** and **Trash** labels.

With a Google Workspace account, Onyx lists every user in the domain and indexes each mailbox.
With a personal Google account, or with an account that has no Workspace admin role, Onyx indexes that one mailbox.

Onyx checks Gmail for new and changed threads every 30 minutes by default.
You can change **Refresh Every** on the connector.

On the Business and Enterprise plans and on Onyx Cloud, set **Document Access** to **Sync with Gmail**.
Each user then sees only the threads from their own mailbox.

## Setting up

You authenticate the connector in one of two ways:

- **OAuth**: sign in with one Google account. Follow the [OAuth guide](./oauth). This works without a Google Workspace.
- **Service Account**: use a Google service account with domain-wide delegation. Follow the
  [Service Account guide](./service_account). A Google Workspace is required.

<Note>
  You can set up more than one Gmail connector on a single Onyx instance, each with its own account.
  This lets you index multiple Google Workspaces from one Onyx deployment.
</Note>

<Note>
  A Google account created in Onyx is listed on both the Gmail and the Google Drive connector forms,
  so one account can back both connectors.
  See [Using one account for Gmail and Google Drive](./oauth#using-one-account-for-gmail-and-google-drive).
</Note>
