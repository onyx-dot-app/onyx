---
title: "Gmail Service Account"
description: "Set up Gmail Service Account for the connector"
---

This guide sets up the Gmail connector with a Google service account. A Google Workspace is required.

If you prefer to sign in with one Google account, follow the [OAuth guide](./oauth).

### Authorization

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Google Cloud project">
    Go to [https://console.cloud.google.com/projectcreate](https://console.cloud.google.com/projectcreate)
    and create a project.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable the Gmail API and the Admin SDK API">
    - In the left panel, open **APIs & services** and go to **Enabled APIs and services**.
    - Click **+ ENABLE APIS AND SERVICES**.
    - Search for **Gmail API** and click **ENABLE**.
    - Search for **Admin SDK API** and click **ENABLE**.

    <img className="rounded-image" src="/assets/admins/connectors/google_drive/GoogleDriveEnableAPI.webp" alt="Google Cloud Console enabling APIs for the project"/>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a service account">
    - Open the [service accounts page](https://console.cloud.google.com/iam-admin/serviceaccounts) in Google Cloud.
    - Click **Create Service Account** and complete step 1. Skip steps 2 and 3.
    - Open the **Keys** section and click **Add Key**. Download the JSON key.

    For Google organizations created after April 2024, allow key creation first:
    - Open this [policy page](https://console.cloud.google.com/iam-admin/orgpolicies/iam-disableServiceAccountKeyCreation).
    - Select **Manage**, then **Override parent's policy**, then **Not enforced** under **Rules**.
    - Select **SET POLICY**.

    <iframe
    width="840" height="473" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/Z5R9HUCVVAE?si=GZxteF6rmIHa1BOE" title="YouTube video
    player" frameBorder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope;
    picture-in-picture; web-share; fullscreen;" allowfullscreen
    ></iframe>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Grant domain-wide delegation">
    Give the service account read-only access to Gmail:
    - Copy the **Unique ID** of the service account.
    - Open the [Domain-wide Delegation page](https://admin.google.com/ac/owl/domainwidedelegation) in the Google Admin
    console.
    - Click **Add new** and enter the **Unique ID** as the client ID.
    - Paste this list into **OAuth scopes**:
    `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly,https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.group.readonly,https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.user.readonly`
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Indexing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the account">
    In the Onyx admin panel, go to **Connectors** and select **Gmail**. Under **Authentication Account**,
    click **Create New** and select **Service Account**.

    - **Primary Admin Email**: the email of a user with an admin role in the Google Workspace that has the
    **Users > Read** and **Groups > Read** privileges. This can be an existing admin or an account created for Onyx,
    such as `onyx-robot@your-domain.com`. Do not enter the service account email.
    Set the role in the Google Admin console under **Account > Admin roles**.
    - **Service Account Key**: upload or paste the JSON key you downloaded.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose document access">
    Under **Document Access**, select who can see the indexed threads.
    **Sync with Gmail** mirrors each user's own mailbox.
    **Specific Groups** and **Sync with Gmail** are available on the Business and Enterprise plans and on Onyx Cloud.
    Then click **Create Connector**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can create more than one Gmail account on a single Onyx instance, each with its own service account.
  This lets you index multiple Google Workspaces from one Onyx deployment.
  Create a separate account and connector for each workspace.
</Note>

<Note>
  A Google account is listed on both the Gmail and the Google Drive connector forms.
  To use one service account for both connectors, delegate the scopes of both:
  `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly,https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly,https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.metadata.readonly,https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.group.readonly,https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.user.readonly`.
  With only one connector's scopes delegated, the account works for that connector only.
</Note>
