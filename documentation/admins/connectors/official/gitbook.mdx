## How it works

The GitBook connector indexes the pages of one GitBook space. Each page becomes one document.
Onyx converts the page content to Markdown and links the document to the page in GitBook.

Onyx checks GitBook for new and changed pages every 30 minutes by default.
You can change **Refresh Every** on the connector.

Permission sync is not available for GitBook.
Everyone who can see the connector's documents in Onyx can see every indexed page.

## Setting up

### Authorization

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the developer settings">
    In GitBook,
    open your account settings and go to **Developer** at
    [https://app.gitbook.com/account/developer](https://app.gitbook.com/account/developer).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create an API token">
    Click **Create new token**, name it, and select the scopes it needs to read the space.
    Click **Create** and copy the token. GitBook does not show it again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<img className="rounded-image" src="/assets/admins/connectors/gitbook/gitbook_1.webp" alt="GitBook developer settings page"/>

### Indexing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the account">
    In the Onyx admin panel, go to **Connectors** and select **GitBook**. Under **Authentication Account**,
    click **Create New** and enter the token as the **API Token**.

    <img className="rounded-image" src="/assets/admins/connectors/gitbook/gitbook_2.webp" alt="GitBook account form with the API Token field"/>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the space ID">
    Enter the **Space ID**. You find it in the URL of a page in the space.
    For `https://app.gitbook.com/o/<organization>/s/<space-id>/`, the space ID is `<space-id>`.

    <img className="rounded-image" src="/assets/admins/connectors/gitbook/gitbook_3.webp" alt="GitBook connector configuration with the Space ID field"/>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose document access">
    Under **Document Access**, select who can see the indexed pages.
    **Specific Groups** is available on the Business and Enterprise plans and on Onyx Cloud.
    Then click **Create Connector**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
