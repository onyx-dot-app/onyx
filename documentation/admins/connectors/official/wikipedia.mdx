## How it works

The Wikipedia connector indexes articles from a Wikipedia language edition. You pick the categories and pages to index.
Each article becomes one document with a link back to Wikipedia.

The connector reads public content only. It needs no credential.

Permission sync is not available. **Document Access** can be **Everyone in Your Organization** or **Specific Groups**.
**Specific Groups** is a paid feature: the Business and Enterprise tiers on Onyx Cloud,
and the Enterprise Edition when self-hosted.

The connector is available in all editions: Community Edition, Business, Enterprise, and Onyx Cloud.

## Setting up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose what to index">
    In the Admin Panel, go to **Connectors** and select **Wikipedia**.
    - **Language Code**: The Wikipedia language edition, for example `en` for English or `de` for German.
    - **Categories to Index**: Category names, without the `Category:` prefix. Onyx indexes every page in each category.
    - **Pages**: Individual page titles to index.
    - **Recursion Depth**: How many levels of subcategories to follow. Enter `0` to index only the listed categories,
    or `-1` to follow all subcategories.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set access and create">
    Under **Connector Settings**, choose **Document Access** and click **Create Connector**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  A high **Recursion Depth** on a large category can pull in many thousands of pages.
  Start small and widen the scope after the first index attempt completes.
</Warning>
