## How it works

Export your Google Site with [Google Takeout](https://takeout.google.com/) and upload the ZIP file to Onyx.
Onyx indexes every HTML page in the export. Each page links back to the live site, based on the base URL you enter.

The upload is a one-time import. Onyx does not poll Google Sites for changes. To index new or changed pages,
export the site again and create a new connector with the new ZIP file.
The Google Sites connector does not support **Sync with Google Sites** (permission sync).

## Setting up

### Exporting Your Google Site

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export with Takeout">
    Follow the guide [here](https://www.steegle.com/google-sites/how-to/export-with-takeout) to export your Google Site.
    Include ONLY the Google Site in the export.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Indexing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the base URL">
    Go to **Admin Panel → Connectors** and select **Google Sites**. This connector does not need a credential.
    In **Base URL**, enter the URL of the root page of your Google Site,
    for example `https://sites.google.com/view/my-site`. Onyx uses it to build the link of each indexed page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload the export">
    In **Site Export**, upload the `.zip` file from the export step.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose document access">
    Under **Document Access**, select **Everyone in Your Organization** or **Specific Groups**.
    **Specific Groups** needs the Business or Enterprise tier on Onyx Cloud, or the Enterprise Edition when self-hosted.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start indexing">
    Select **Create Connector**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
