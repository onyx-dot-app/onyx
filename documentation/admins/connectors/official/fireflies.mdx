## How it works

The Fireflies connector indexes the meeting transcripts in your Fireflies account. Each meeting becomes one document.
The document holds the transcript, one entry per sentence with the speaker's name. It also carries the meeting title,
date, organizer, and participants.

Onyx checks Fireflies for new transcripts every 30 minutes by default.
You can change **Refresh Every** on the connector. Each run also rereads recent meetings,
so a transcript that Fireflies finished late is still picked up.

Permission sync is not available for Fireflies.
Everyone who can see the connector's documents in Onyx can see every indexed transcript.

## Setting up

### Authorization

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Developer Settings">
    Log in to Fireflies with an account that can see the meetings you want to index. Click your profile avatar,
    select **Settings**, then **Developer Settings**.

    ![Fireflies settings menu](/assets/admins/connectors/fireflies/fireflies1.webp)

    <img className="rounded-image" src="/assets/admins/connectors/fireflies/fireflies2.webp" alt="Developer Settings in Fireflies"/>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Copy the API key">
    Copy the **API Key**.

    <img className="rounded-image" src="/assets/admins/connectors/fireflies/fireflies3.webp" alt="Fireflies API key"/>
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Indexing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the account">
    In the Onyx admin panel, go to **Connectors** and select **Fireflies**. Under **Authentication Account**,
    click **Create New** and enter the key as the **API Token**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose document access">
    Under **Document Access**, select who can see the indexed transcripts.
    **Specific Groups** is available on the Business and Enterprise plans and on Onyx Cloud.
    Then click **Create Connector**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
