## How it works

The MediaWiki connector indexes pages from any public MediaWiki site, for example a company wiki or a Fandom wiki.
You choose the pages to index by category, by page name, or both. Each wiki page becomes one document.
The page categories are stored as document metadata.

The connector reads the wiki through its public API. It does not need a credential. On each poll,
the connector re-indexes pages that changed since the last run.

<Note>
  To index Wikipedia, use the [Wikipedia connector](/admins/connectors/official/wikipedia) instead.
</Note>

## Setting up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the site details">
    Go to **Admin Panel → Connectors** and select **MediaWiki**.

    - **Language Code**: the language code of the wiki, for example `en`. The default is `en`.
    - **MediaWiki Site URL**: the URL of the wiki, for example `https://wiki.example.com`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose what to index">
    - **Categories to Index**: category names to index, with or without the `Category:` prefix. The connector indexes every page in each category.
    - **Pages**: names of individual pages to index.
    - **Recursion Depth**: how many levels of subcategories to follow. `0` indexes only the listed categories. `-1` follows every subcategory.

    Enter at least one category or page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose document access">
    Under **Document Access**, select **Everyone in Your Organization** or **Specific Groups**.
    **Specific Groups** needs the Business or Enterprise tier on Onyx Cloud, or the Enterprise Edition when self-hosted.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start indexing">
    Select **Create Connector** to start indexing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The MediaWiki connector does not support **Sync with MediaWiki** (permission sync).
</Note>
