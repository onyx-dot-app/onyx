## How it works

The Freshdesk connector indexes the tickets in your Freshdesk account.
Each ticket becomes one document with the ticket description as its text. The document carries the subject, status,
priority, source, type, and the contact emails as metadata.

Onyx checks Freshdesk for new and updated tickets every 30 minutes by default.
You can change **Refresh Every** on the connector.

Permission sync is not available for Freshdesk.
Everyone who can see the connector's documents in Onyx can see every indexed ticket.

## Setting up

### Authorization

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile settings">
    Log in to Freshdesk as a verified agent, admin, or account owner.
    Click your profile avatar and select **Profile settings**.

    <img className="rounded-image" src="/assets/admins/connectors/freshdesk/freshdesk_1.webp" alt="Freshdesk profile menu"/>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Copy the API key">
    Select **View API Key** and copy the key.

    <img className="rounded-image" src="/assets/admins/connectors/freshdesk/freshdesk_2.webp" alt="Freshdesk API key in profile settings"/>
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Indexing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the account">
    In the Onyx admin panel, go to **Connectors** and select **Freshdesk**. Under **Authentication Account**,
    click **Create New**. Enter the **Freshdesk Domain**, for example `acme` for `acme.freshdesk.com`,
    and the key as the **API Token**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose document access">
    Under **Document Access**, select who can see the indexed tickets.
    **Specific Groups** is available on the Business and Enterprise plans and on Onyx Cloud.
    Then click **Create Connector**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
