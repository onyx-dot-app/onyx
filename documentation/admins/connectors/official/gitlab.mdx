## How it works

The GitLab connector indexes one GitLab project. It indexes:

- **Merge requests**: open and closed, with the title and description.
- **Issues**: open and closed, including incidents, with the title and description.

Each item carries its URL, state, and type as metadata. The connector does not index comments, diffs, or commits.

Onyx checks GitLab for new and changed items every 30 minutes by default.
You can change **Refresh Every** on the connector.

Permission sync is not available for GitLab.
Everyone who can see the connector's documents in Onyx can see every indexed item.

## Setting up

### Authorization

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your access tokens">
    Log in to GitLab. In the left sidebar, select your avatar, then **Edit profile**.
    Select **Access tokens** and then **Add new token**.

    <video className="rounded-image" src="/assets/admins/connectors/gitlab/GitlabConnectorAuth.mp4" autoPlay muted loop playsInline aria-label="Creating a GitLab personal access token with the read_api scope" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the token details">
    Enter a name and an expiry date. The token expires at midnight UTC on that date. If you leave the date empty,
    GitLab sets it to 365 days from today. When the token expires, indexing stops until you save a new token in Onyx.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the scope and create the token">
    Select the `read_api` scope.
    See the [GitLab scope
    reference](https://docs.gitlab.com/ee/user/profile/personal_access_tokens.html#personal-access-token-scopes)
    for details. Select **Create personal access token** and copy the token.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Indexing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the account">
    In the Onyx admin panel, go to **Connectors** and select **GitLab**. Under **Authentication Account**,
    click **Create New**. Enter the **GitLab URL** and the token as the **API Token**.
    Keep `https://gitlab.com` for GitLab.com. Enter the address of your instance for a self-hosted GitLab.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the project">
    Enter the **Project Owner** and the **Project Name**. For `https://gitlab.com/onyx-ai/onyx`,
    the owner is `onyx-ai` and the name is `onyx`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the content types">
    **Include MRs** and **Include Issues** are both on by default. Turn one off to skip that content type.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose document access">
    Under **Document Access**, select who can see the indexed items.
    **Specific Groups** is available on the Business and Enterprise plans and on Onyx Cloud.
    Then click **Create Connector**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Indexing code files

Self-hosted deployments can also index the text files in the project.
Set the `GITLAB_CONNECTOR_INCLUDE_CODE_FILES` environment variable to `true` and restart Onyx.
See [Configuration](/deployment/configuration/configuration).
The setting applies to every GitLab connector in the deployment.
