## How it works

The Dropbox connector indexes the files in the Dropbox account of the access token. It walks every folder from the root.
You cannot limit the connector to specific folders.

Onyx indexes the supported document types and skips files larger than 20 MB. For each file,
Onyx creates or reuses a Dropbox shared link and uses it as the document link.

After the first run, Onyx checks Dropbox for new and changed files every 30 minutes by default.
You can change **Refresh Every** on the connector.

Permission sync is not available for Dropbox.
Everyone who can see the connector's documents in Onyx can see every indexed file.

## Setting up

### Authorization

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Dropbox app">
    Go to [https://www.dropbox.com/developers/apps](https://www.dropbox.com/developers/apps) and click **Create app**.
    Select **Scoped access** and **Full Dropbox** as the type of access.
    Give your app a name like `Onyx Connector` and click **Create app**.

    <img className="rounded-image" src="/assets/admins/connectors/dropbox/dropbox-connector-1.webp" alt="Dropbox app creation form with Scoped access and Full Dropbox selected"/>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Grant the required scopes">
    On the **Permissions** tab, check `files.content.read` and `sharing.write`.
    Onyx needs them to read files and to create document links. Click **Submit**.

    <img className="rounded-image" src="/assets/admins/connectors/dropbox/dropbox-connector-2.webp" alt="Permissions tab of a Dropbox app"/>

    <img className="rounded-image" src="/assets/admins/connectors/dropbox/dropbox-connector-3.webp" alt="Dropbox app permissions with files.content.read and sharing.write checked"/>

    <img className="rounded-image" src="/assets/admins/connectors/dropbox/dropbox-connector-4.webp" alt="Dropbox banner asking you to click Submit to save permission changes"/>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Generate an access token">
    Open the **Settings** tab and scroll down to the **OAuth 2** section.
    If the **Access token expiration** setting is available, set it to **No expiration**.
    Click **Generate** and copy the access token. Refresh the page if you need to generate it again.

    <Warning>
      Grant the permissions before you generate the token. A token does not pick up permissions that you grant later.
      Onyx does not refresh the token. A short-lived token stops working after a few hours.
    </Warning>

    <img className="rounded-image" src="/assets/admins/connectors/dropbox/dropbox-connector-5.webp" alt="Generated Dropbox access token"/>
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Indexing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the account">
    In the Onyx admin panel, go to **Connectors** and select **Dropbox**. Under **Authentication Account**,
    click **Create New** and enter the access token as the **API Token**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose document access">
    Under **Document Access**, select who can see the indexed files.
    **Specific Groups** is available on the Business and Enterprise plans and on Onyx Cloud.
    Then click **Create Connector**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
