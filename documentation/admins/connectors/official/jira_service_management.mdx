## How it works

Onyx indexes the **customer requests** (portal tickets) in your Jira Service
Management project. Every request becomes one searchable document in Onyx:
its summary, description, and **public** comments. Internal agent notes are
not indexed.

Onyx pulls new and changed requests on the connector's **Refresh Every**
schedule. The default is **30 minutes**.

This connector is for Jira Service Management (Cloud) service desks. For
Jira Software issue tracking, use the **Jira** connector instead.

## Setting up

### Authorization

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an Atlassian API token">
    Go to
    [id.atlassian.com/manage-profile/security/api-tokens](https://id.atlassian.com/manage-profile/security/api-tokens),
    select **Create API token**, and copy the token. Use an Atlassian account
    whose JSM agent role can read the service desks you want indexed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Indexing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an authentication account">
    In Onyx, go to **Admin Panel → Connectors** and select **Jira Service
    Management**. Under **Authentication Account**, select **Create New**.
    Enter a **Display Name**, your Atlassian site URL
    (for example `https://your-domain.atlassian.net`), the account's **email
    address**, and the **API token** from the previous step. Select **Create**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure the connector">
    Under **Connector Settings**, give the connector a **Display Name**. In
    **Service Desk ID**, enter the numeric ID of one service desk to index
    (visible in the desk's URL, for example the `7` in
    `.../servicedesk/customer/portal/7`). Leave it empty to index every desk
    the account can read.

    Under **Document Access**, choose who can see the indexed documents.
    **Everyone in Your Organization** shows them to all Onyx users.
    **Specific Groups** limits them to the Onyx user groups you pick.
    **Specific Groups** is a paid feature: the Business and Enterprise tiers
    on Onyx Cloud, and the Enterprise Edition when self-hosted. The Jira
    Service Management connector does not sync JSM permissions.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Start indexing">
    Select **Create Connector** to save the connector and start indexing.
    Open **Admin Panel → Indexing Status** and select the connector to follow
    the first indexing run.
  </Step>
</Steps>
