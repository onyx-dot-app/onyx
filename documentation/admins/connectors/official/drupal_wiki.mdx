## How it works

The Drupal Wiki connector indexes the pages of your Drupal Wiki instance.
You can index every space the account can read, or only specific spaces and pages. You can also index page attachments.

Onyx converts each page to text and keeps the space and author as document metadata. After the first run,
Onyx checks Drupal Wiki for new and changed pages every 30 minutes by default.
You can change **Refresh Every** on the connector. Pruning removes pages that were deleted in Drupal Wiki.

The connector indexes only the content that the API token can read. Permission sync is not available for Drupal Wiki.
Everyone who can see the connector's documents in Onyx can see every indexed page.

## Setting up

### Authorization

You need a Drupal Wiki personal access token. The token starts with `pat:`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the REST API">
    Make sure the REST API is enabled in your Drupal Wiki instance.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Generate an access token">
    Follow the Drupal Wiki documentation to generate a personal access token:
    [https://help.drupal-wiki.com/node/605#2-Zugriffs-Token-generieren](https://help.drupal-wiki.com/node/605#2-Zugriffs-Token-generieren)
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Indexing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the account">
    In the Onyx admin panel, go to **Connectors** and select **Drupal Wiki**. Under **Authentication Account**,
    click **Create New**. Enter the **Base URL** of your instance, for example `https://help.drupal-wiki.com`,
    and the token as the **API Token**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the content to index">
    Under **Drupal Wiki Content to Index**, select a tab:
    - **Everything**: Onyx indexes all pages in every space that the account can read.
    - **Specific Spaces/Pages**: Enter **Space IDs** and **Page IDs**. Use numeric IDs only. To find space IDs, see
    [https://help.drupal-wiki.com/node/606](https://help.drupal-wiki.com/node/606).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the attachment option">
    Check **Include Attachments** to also index the files attached to pages.
    Onyx indexes the supported document types and skips attachments larger than 10 MB.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose document access">
    Under **Document Access**, select who can see the indexed pages.
    **Specific Groups** is available on the Business and Enterprise plans and on Onyx Cloud.
    Then click **Create Connector**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
