## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a plan">
    Go to **Admin Panel > Plans & Billing** and click **Get Business Plan**,
    or **Contact Sales** for Enterprise pricing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your billing cycle">
    Select **Monthly** or **Annual** billing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set your seat count">
    Enter the number of seats you need. The minimum is your current number of active users.
    Service accounts and users who exist only for permission sync do not use a seat.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete payment">
    Click **Continue to Payment** to open Stripe checkout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  New Business subscriptions include a **1-month free trial**. You won't be charged until the trial ends. On Cloud,
  add a payment method before the trial ends. You can also end the trial early with **Upgrade now**.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Enterprise plans are managed through sales.
  The billing page then shows **Your plan is managed through sales** with a link to contact billing for changes.
</Note>

## Self-Hosted License Activation

On **Onyx Cloud**, your subscription is active immediately after checkout.

On **self-hosted** deployments, a **license** is automatically claimed and stored on your instance after checkout.
The license is cryptographically signed and validated locally,
so your instance does not need to phone home on every request.

<Note>
  If your self-hosted deployment cannot reach the internet,
  you can [upload a license file manually](/admins/billing/manage#air-gapped-license-activation)
  instead of going through Stripe checkout.
</Note>
