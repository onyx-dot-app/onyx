## Admin Panel

Click your profile icon and select **Admin Panel**.

<Note>
  The first user in your Onyx deployment is automatically an Admin. Only Admins can make other users Admins.
</Note>

<img className="rounded-image" src="/assets/admins/admin_panel_overview.webp" alt="Onyx Admin Panel Overview"/>

## Editions

Some Admin Panel pages depend on your edition. Onyx has four editions:

- **Community Edition (CE)**: free, self-hosted, no license.
- **Business**: adds groups, permission sync, SSO, usage dashboards, theming, and the LLM Gateway.
- **Enterprise**: adds SCIM, custom analytics, hook extensions, and other advanced controls.
- **Cloud**: hosted by Onyx with Business or Enterprise features depending on your plan.

Pages that need a higher edition stay visible in the sidebar with a lock.
See [Plans & Billing](/admins/billing/overview) for details.
