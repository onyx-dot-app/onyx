White labeling settings are on the **Appearance & Theming** page in the **Admin Panel**, under **Organization**.

<Info>
  **Business Plan Feature**

  Appearance & Theming requires a Business plan or higher. The custom help link requires an Enterprise plan.
  Onyx Cloud includes all of them. [View plans](/admins/billing/overview).
</Info>

Change the settings, then click **Apply Changes**. The changes apply to all users.

## Logo and Name

- **Application Logo** — upload an image to replace the Onyx logo in the sidebar and on the login page.
- **Application Display Name** — set a name that replaces "Onyx" across the app.
- **Logo Display Style** — choose **Logo & Name**, **Logo Only**, or **Name Only** for the top of the sidebar.
  The options become available after you add a logo or a name.

## Messaging

- **Greeting Message** — a short message on the home page.
- **Chat Header Text** — text in the header of the chat interface.
- **Chat Footer Text** — Markdown content in the footer, for disclaimers or extra information.
- **Login Page Subtitle** — replaces the tagline under the welcome heading on the login page.
- **Custom Help Link** and **Custom Help Link Label** — add a link to your own help resource in the user menu,
  next to the Onyx documentation. Enterprise plan only.

<img className="rounded-image" src="/assets/admins/advanced_configs/whitelabeling_message_header_footer.webp" alt="Greeting Message, Chat Header Text, and Chat Footer Text"/>

## System Announcement

**System Announcement** shows a persistent banner that users can dismiss.
Turn on **Pop-up Notice at First Visit** to also show it as a full-screen pop-up to every user at their next visit.

## First Visit Notice

Show a one-time notice to new users at their first visit.

| Option | Description |
|---|---|
| **Show First Visit Notice** | Turn the notice on or off |
| **Notice Header** | Title at the top of the notice |
| **Notice Content** | Body text of the notice |
| **Require Consent to Notice** | Users must read and agree before they can use Onyx |
| **Notice Consent Prompt** | The text next to the consent checkbox |
