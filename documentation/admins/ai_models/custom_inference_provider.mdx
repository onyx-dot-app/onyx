If Onyx does not list your provider, you can still connect it.
Onyx offers two options under **Self-hosted & Custom** on the **Language Models** page:

- **OpenAI-Compatible**: for any endpoint that implements the OpenAI Chat Completions API.
  Onyx reads the model list from the endpoint. Use this option when possible.
- **Custom Models**: for any provider that [LiteLLM](https://docs.litellm.ai/docs/providers) supports.
  You enter the LiteLLM provider ID and list the models by hand.

## OpenAI-Compatible

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find Your Endpoint">
    Determine the base URL of your provider. It looks like `https://yourprovider.com/v1` or `http://localhost:12345/v1`.

    Onyx adds `/v1` to the URL if it is missing.
  </Step>

  <NavigateToAiModels />

  <Step title="Connect the Endpoint">
    Under **Self-hosted & Custom**, click **Connect** on the **OpenAI-Compatible** card.

    Enter the **API Base URL**.

    If your endpoint requires authentication, paste the **API Key**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Load Your Models">
    In the **Models** section, click **Refresh models**. Onyx reads the models from the endpoint's `/v1/models` route.
  </Step>

  <StandardAiModelConfigs />

  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect**. Onyx tests the endpoint with one of the selected models before it saves the provider.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Custom Models (LiteLLM)

Use this option for a provider that is not OpenAI-compatible but that LiteLLM supports.

<Steps>
  <NavigateToAiModels />

  <Step title="Set Up a Custom Provider">
    Under **Self-hosted & Custom**, click **Set Up** on the **Custom Models** card.

    Give the provider a **Display Name**.

    In the **Provider** field,
    enter the provider ID as shown in the [LiteLLM provider list](https://docs.litellm.ai/docs/providers),
    for example `gemini` or `mistral`.

    <img className="rounded-image" src="/assets/admins/ai_models/litellm_provider_name.webp" alt="Custom Models setup dialog with the Provider field"/>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter Credentials and Endpoint Details">
    Paste the **API Key** if the provider requires one.

    Fill in the optional **API Base URL** and **API Version** fields if the provider needs them.

    Under **Environment Variables**, add any extra properties the provider needs, for example `OPENAI_ORGANIZATION`.
    Onyx passes them to LiteLLM as environment variables.
  </Step>

  <Step title="List Your Models">
    In the **Models** table, click **Add Model** for each model you want to make available.
    Enter the **Model Name** as LiteLLM expects it. Optionally, set a **Display Name**,
    the **Input Type** (**Text Only** or **Text & Image**), and **Max Tokens**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Models Access">
    Under **Models Access**, choose **All Users & Agents** or **Named Groups & Agents**.

    With **Named Groups & Agents**, only the groups and agents you add can use this provider.
    See [Language Model Access Controls](/admins/advanced_configs/llm_access_controls).
    Group-based access requires the Business tier or higher.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect**. Onyx tests the provider with one of the listed models before it saves it.
  </Step>
</Steps>
