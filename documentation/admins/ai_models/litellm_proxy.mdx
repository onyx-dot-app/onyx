## Guide

Configure Onyx to use models exposed by your LiteLLM Proxy server.

<Note>
  Onyx reads the model list from your LiteLLM Proxy.
  This is a good option when you want one gateway in front of multiple providers or self-hosted backends.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set Up LiteLLM Proxy">
    Deploy LiteLLM Proxy and make sure it is reachable from the Onyx server.

    You need the LiteLLM Proxy **API Base URL** and an **API Key** (a LiteLLM virtual key or the master key).

    The local default is commonly `http://localhost:4000`.
  </Step>

  <NavigateToAiModels />

  <Step title="Connect LiteLLM Proxy">
    Under **Gateways & Routers**, click **Connect** on the **LiteLLM Proxy** card.

    Enter the proxy **API Base URL** and paste your **API Key**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Load Your Models">
    In the **Models** section, click **Refresh models**. Onyx lists the models that your LiteLLM Proxy currently routes,
    including their context size when the proxy reports it.
  </Step>

  <StandardAiModelConfigs />

  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect**. Onyx tests the proxy with one of the selected models before it saves the provider.
  </Step>
</Steps>
