## Guide

<Steps>
  <NavigateToAiModels />

  <Step title="Connect Anthropic">
    Under **Add Provider**, click **Connect** on the **Claude** (Anthropic) card.

    Create an [API key](https://console.anthropic.com/dashboard)
    in the Anthropic Console and paste it in the **API Key** field.

    <img className="rounded-image" src="/assets/admins/ai_models/anthropic_config.webp" alt="Anthropic provider setup dialog"/>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Models">
    Onyx lists the recommended Claude models. Keep **Auto Update** on to add new Claude models when they are released.
  </Step>

  <StandardAiModelConfigs />

  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect**. Onyx tests the API key with one of the selected models before it saves the provider.
  </Step>
</Steps>
