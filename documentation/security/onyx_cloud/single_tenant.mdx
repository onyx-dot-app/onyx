## Hosting

The Onyx single-tenant cloud gives you complete data separation. Onyx can host it in any region of your choice.
There are no additional third parties or subprocessors.

You can choose how the deployment is run:

- **Fully managed**: The Onyx team runs the deployment on AWS and handles upgrades, scaling, and monitoring.
- **Self-managed**: Your team runs the deployment in your own cloud account, with support from Onyx.

Onyx sizes the deployment for your team and auto-scales it for high availability during peaks.

Onyx offers a single-instance deployment or a Kubernetes deployment, based on your expected usage and scale.

## Access

If you want access to the Onyx single-tenant cloud without a demo, email [hello@onyx.app](mailto:hello@onyx.app) with:
- Your organization name
