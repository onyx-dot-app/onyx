
Onyx has three editions. **Community Edition** is free and open source.
**Business** and **Enterprise** add features for security, user management, and site customization.

<Tip>
  Onyx Cloud includes every Business and Enterprise feature.
</Tip>

## Who needs Business or Enterprise?

- Organizations with more than 100 users
- Environments that need group-scoped access or several SSO providers
- Companies with strict security and compliance requirements
- Teams that need centralized user management and analytics
- Deployments that inherit permissions from external systems
