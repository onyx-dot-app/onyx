## Our Mission

Our mission is to make Generative AI and knowledge accessible to every team worldwide.
As large language models—both proprietary and open-source—become more widespread,
Onyx serves as the interface to their knowledge and reasoning capabilities.

Onyx makes the LLM more useful by giving it context (from the web and from your team),
built-in Actions like Internal Search, Web Search, and Code Interpreter,
and Plugins that connect it to your applications.

## Why Open Source?

<Card title="Security" icon="shield-check">
  We believe teams should feel safe with the AI platform that they adopt. That's why Onyx is fully transparent.
  You know exactly how your data is processed and stored, and all of this happens within your Onyx deployment.
</Card>

<Card title="Flexibility" icon="puzzle-piece">
  GenAI is a fundamental part of modern company strategy, so we want to offer the most flexible and future-proof option.
  Every team is unique in its needs, so we build Onyx with an emphasis on customizability,
  both for building on top of the platform and for modifying it directly.
</Card>

<Card title="Control" icon="hand-fist">
  Open source also gives control to your team. The GenAI space changes rapidly,
  and your vendor of choice may not always be aligned with your team's direction.
  OSS combines the benefits of building in-house with getting product updates for free.
  Open source also prevents lock-in, so you can use any LLM provider (or your own LLM).
</Card>

## Commercial Viability

We are a team of builders and we want as many people as possible to find value in our software.

To borrow from Sid Sijbrandij (Co-Founder, GitLab):
> "We need to think in the interests of the project, while tending to the realities of running a business to support it... After all, we know that to sustain the project, we need to make it commercially viable."

<Note>
  Every organization, regardless of size or budget,
  has access to the core capabilities of the Onyx Community Edition for free. This includes Chat, Internal Search,
  Web Search, Deep Research, custom Agents, Code Interpreter, Image Generation, Projects, and Plugins.
</Note>

For organizations with additional requirements, we offer **Business** and **Enterprise** plans.
These add features such as Craft, single sign-on (OIDC/SAML), permission sync, user groups and access controls,
usage analytics, and white-labeling. They are available both self-hosted and on Onyx Cloud.
See [Enterprise Edition](/admins/getting_started/enterprise_edition).

The paid plans sustain our mission and let us keep the large majority of Onyx's features free and open for everyone.

## On Contributions

Onyx is built in collaboration with our community and we welcome contributions.
Our team works to keep the Onyx project easy for teams to contribute to or to maintain in-house. This comes down to:

- Intentional high-level architecture.
- Strong product direction and refusal to accept bloat for niche use cases.
- A consistent and high quality of engineering best practices across the code base.

To that end,
we ask that contributors start with smaller issues and get approval from the maintainers for larger feature
contributions.
